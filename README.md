## 🌱 About Me

Hello! I'm Ana, a product-focused fullstack engineer and tech lead.

What I enjoy most is understanding the "why" behind a feature before jumping into the "how" and helping teams build products that are faster to ship and easier to maintain.

Over the past 10+ years working with React, TypeScript and Node.js, I've had the chance to build products for **BMW Group**, **Gympass/Wellhub**, **Zenklub** and **VTEX**.

I've lived in different countries, and working with people from so many backgrounds taught me that good engineering is as much about communication and empathy as it is about code.

I also love mentoring and seeing engineers grow into full ownership of their work.

## 📚 What You'll Find Here

This repository is my personal blog, where I share notes and lessons learned about:

- ⚛️ **React** – building interfaces that are pleasant to use and to maintain
- 🔷 **TypeScript** – writing code that is safer and easier to reason about
- 🟢 **Node.js** – APIs, tooling and everything on the server side
- 🏗️ **Architecture** – shipping fast without sacrificing maintainability
- 🤝 **Mentoring & leadership** – helping engineers and teams grow

Feel free to explore, open an issue with questions or suggestions, and learn along with me.
